Common Hong Kong 18 District Datasets
These datasets provide the common geographic and demographic foundation for all project sectors. They allow hospital, elderly services, libraries, recreation facilities, and public transport resources to be compared consistently across Hong Kong’s 18 districts.

There are two main types of common datasets:

1. Population Census Data
2016 Population By-census: Statistics and Boundaries of District Council Districts
2021 Population Census: Statistics and Boundaries of District Council Districts
These datasets provide comprehensive district-level information, including population size, age, sex, household income, employment status, education, and housing characteristics. The data can be used to normalize resource provision, such as calculating the number of facilities per 10,000 residents, and to examine relationships between public-resource distribution and demographic or socioeconomic conditions.

Using both the 2016 and 2021 datasets also allows the group to analyze changes in population characteristics and resource demand over time.

2. District Boundary Data
Hong Kong Administrative Boundaries
Hong Kong 18 District Boundary Data from the Common Spatial Data Infrastructure Portal
These geographic datasets define the boundaries of Hong Kong’s 18 districts. They will be used to assign facilities and transport services to their corresponding districts and to create choropleth maps and other district-based visualizations.

The same district boundaries will be used across all sectors to ensure that comparisons are geographically consistent.

Public Transport Datasets
The public transport datasets contain geographic network data and service statistics for Hong Kong’s bus and railway systems. They will be combined with census data to evaluate the distribution, accessibility, and adequacy of public transport in each district.

There are two main types of public transport data:

1. Geographic Data
Bus routes
Coordinates of bus stop locations
Railways of Hong Kong
Routes and fares of public transport in GeoJSON format
These datasets provide the geographic locations and network structures of bus routes, bus stops, and railway lines. They will be used to create maps showing the spatial distribution and coverage of public transport across the 18 districts.

The geographic analysis can identify districts with dense or limited transport coverage. It can also examine relationships between transport availability and district population, employment, income, and other demographic characteristics.

2. Service and Statistical Data
MTR routes, fares, and barrier-free facilities
Routes and fares of public transport
Monthly Traffic and Transport Digest
These datasets provide information about routes, fares, accessibility facilities, passenger usage, and changes in transport activity over time. They will support visualizations such as bar charts, time-series charts, accessibility comparisons, and per-capita transport indicators.

By combining these datasets with district population and socioeconomic data, the project can compare:

The number of MTR stations, bus stops, and routes in each district
The number of residents served by each station, stop, or route
Differences in transport availability between districts
Relationships between transport provision, population, household income, and employment
Districts that may have insufficient transport coverage or accessibility facilities



## Public Transport Methodology

Python will be used to process the datasets and create two main types of visualizations: geographic maps and statistical charts.

### 1. Geographic Visualizations

GeoPandas, Folium, and Plotly will be used to create geographic maps showing the distribution of public transport across Hong Kong’s 18 districts. District-boundary data will be combined with the locations of bus stops, bus routes, MTR stations, and railway lines.

The geographic visualizations will include:

- Interactive maps of bus stops, bus routes, MTR stations, and railway lines  
- Choropleth maps showing the number of transport facilities in each district  
- Per-capita maps showing bus stops, routes, or MTR stations per 10,000 residents  
- Accessibility maps identifying districts with relatively limited transport coverage  

These maps will help identify differences in transport distribution and possible underserved areas.

### 2. Statistical Visualizations

Pandas will be used to calculate district-level indicators, while Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly will be used to create statistical charts.

The statistical visualizations will include:

- Bar charts comparing transport facilities and routes across districts  
- Scatter plots examining relationships between transport provision and population, income, or employment  
- Time-series charts showing changes in passenger usage and transport activity  
- Charts comparing the availability of barrier-free MTR facilities  
- Supply–demand charts identifying districts with large populations but limited transport resources  

The geographic maps and statistical charts will be combined to evaluate whether public transport resources are distributed fairly and sufficiently across Hong Kong’s districts. The final visualizations may be presented in an interactive dashboard using Streamlit or Dash.
